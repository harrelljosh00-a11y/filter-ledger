# Content Strategy

## Content pillars
1. **Know your water:** reading your utility's Consumer Confidence Report (CCR), testing well water, what "chloramine" means. This is the trust builder and the first step in every funnel.
2. **Certification decoded:** NSF/ANSI 42/53/58/401 explained, "tested to" vs "certified", how to look up a model.
3. **Filter by contaminant:** lead, PFAS (PFOA/PFOS), chloramine, fluoride, microplastics claims, hardness.
4. **Filter by type:** pitcher, faucet, under-sink, countertop RO, under-sink RO, whole-house, shower, fridge.
5. **Cost of ownership:** annual filter cost, cost per gallon, waste water of RO systems.
6. **Comparisons:** "X vs Y", built only on listed certifications and published specs.

## Page templates (built in the site)
| Template | Purpose | Required blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Guide | Education, top of funnel | TL;DR, sources, "what to do next" |
| Roundup | "Best X for Y" | Disclosure, method box, picks with Claim Check badges, cost table, FAQ |
| Comparison | "X vs Y" | Side-by-side certification table, verdict by situation |
| Review | Single product | Claim Check table, cost of ownership, who it's for and not for, alternatives |
| Article | News/explainers (e.g., EPA rule changes) | Date, sources, what changes for you |

## Every-page checklist (the pipeline)
1. Topic and reader problem defined. 2. Primary query and intent noted. 3. Specs from the manufacturer page (URL recorded). 4. Certifications looked up on NSF / WQA / IAPMO listings (URL recorded). 5. Prices marked "at time of writing" with the date. 6. Draft. 7. Claim-language audit (BRAND_GUIDELINES.md). 8. Disclosure present. 9. Internal links (2+ in, 2+ out). 10. `lastVerified` set. 11. Published and logged.

## Facebook (primary channel)
- **Formats:** (a) Wren Reels, 30–45 s "Claim Check" explainers; (b) carousel/photo posts like "3 things your water report tells you"; (c) a weekly roundup post.
- **Starting cadence (hypothesis):** 1 Reel + 1 post per day, reviewed after 14 days.
- **Link placement:** post with the link in the first comment, or Meta's affiliate tagging where eligible. Test both.
- **Every post has one job:** reach, trust, educate, engage, or click. The job is tagged in the calendar.
- **Labels:** "AI info" on all Wren videos; branded-content/affiliate disclosure on link posts.

## 90-day SEO roadmap (long-tail first)
**Month 1 (foundations, 10 pages):** NSF standards explained ✅ (drafted) · How to read your water quality report · Certified vs tested-to · Best filters certified for lead (by type) · Pitcher vs faucet vs under-sink · Chloramine: which filters are certified · RO systems: waste water explained · Cost of ownership calculator page · Well water testing first steps · EPA PFAS rule 2026: what changed for homeowners.
**Month 2 (comparisons, 10 pages):** "[Brand] [model] vs [model]" for the most-searched pairs *after* checking Search Console queries; "best under-sink filter for PFAS"; "best shower filter (what certifications exist)".
**Month 3 (scale what works):** expand clusters with impressions but low clicks; refresh any page whose certifications changed.

Target query patterns: "is [model] NSF certified", "[model] vs [model]", "filter certified for [contaminant]", "does [type] remove [contaminant]", "[model] replacement filter cost".

## Repurposing
One page produces 1 Reel, 1 carousel and 1 email section. Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are added after the Facebook workflow is stable (week 4+). Pinterest gets tested in month 2 (kitchen and home content fits it).
