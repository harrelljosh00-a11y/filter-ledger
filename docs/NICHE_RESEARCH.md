# Niche Research

_Last updated: 2026-11-26 · Owner: Claude (operator) · Approver: Josh_

## Method

21 niches scored 1–5 on 11 weighted criteria (weights sum to 100). Weighted score = Σ(weight × score) / 5.

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 means |
|---|---|---|
| intent | 15 | Shoppers actively compare models before buying |
| commission | 15 | High rate × average order value (AOV) = meaningful $ per sale |
| programs | 10 | Many reputable programs, including direct merchant programs, not just Amazon |
| low_competition | 12 | Top results are not all locked up by big publishers |
| seo_small_site | 10 | Long-tail queries a new site can realistically rank for |
| fb_video_fit | 12 | Fits Facebook's audience (older, homeowners) and short explainer videos |
| repeat | 8 | Built-in repeat purchases (refills, consumables) |
| trust_brand | 7 | Room for a trusted, recognizable editorial brand |
| scalability | 5 | Content can be produced at volume without hands-on testing |
| low_risk | 3 | Low regulatory, health-claim and safety exposure |
| stable_season | 3 | Year-round demand |

**What's verified and what's estimated.** Commission rates, cookie windows and program rules cited below were checked against public program pages or network listings on 2026-11-26. The 1–5 scores are **my judgment** from that evidence and are **not** measured search-volume data: we have no paid SEO tool and aren't buying one. Once the site is live, Search Console impressions will replace these judgments.

## Evidence behind the key scores (verified 2026-11-26)

**Amazon Associates (baseline for every niche)**
- Rates are fixed by category. The official schedule lists 10% for Luxury Beauty and 4% for "All Other Categories". Secondary summaries put Home, Pets, Sports, Furniture, Baby and Tools at 3%.
- The standard Amazon session (cookie) is usually 24 hours.
- Amazon reviews a new account only after **3 qualifying sales within 180 days**, and personal orders don't count. Sites need "robust original content", with about 10 posts as a rule of thumb.
- The April 14, 2026 agreement update defines original content as requiring commentary, analysis or transformation. It also disqualifies purchases referred through paid or boosted ads that link to Amazon.
- **Implication:** Amazon is a weak primary program here (low rate, 24-hour window, approval gate). Direct merchant programs matter more.

**Home water filtration (rank 1)**
- Waterdrop's direct program states at least 8% commission, AOV above $235 and a 3.5% average conversion rate. Its UK program on Awin lists 7% with a 30-day cookie.
- Aquasana states commissions starting at 10%. A PartnerCentric case study cites a 10% baseline and $129 AOV.
- AquaTru advertises up to 22% commission on its own page. Network listings show 5% on Impact, so the real rate needs confirming on approval.
- Clearly Filtered is listed on FlexOffers with a 45-day cookie.
- Demand driver: on May 18, 2026 EPA proposed keeping the 4 ppt PFOA/PFOS limits, letting utilities opt into a 2031 deadline (from 2029), and rescinding limits for four other PFAS. Monitoring still starts April 2027. Consumers are confused, and confusion drives research.
- Repeat purchases: replacement cartridges on a 2–12 month cycle.
- Facebook fit: homeowners and parents aged 30–65, which is Facebook's core demographic.

**Home espresso (rank 2)**
- Breville runs on Impact with up to 8% commission and a 30-day cookie (Breville AU program page). Pro Coffee Gear pays 3% rising to 5%, and 7% on Rocket and Fiorenzato.
- High AOV and very visual, but the space is crowded with established YouTube reviewers and big publishers.

**3D printing (rank 3)**
- Bambu Lab on Awin has a 15-day cookie, and its rates vary by product (not published). LayerLine (Awin) pays 5% with a 30-day cookie.
- Strong community and long-tail search, but the audience skews toward YouTube and Reddit more than Facebook.

**Portable power stations (rank 5)**
- EcoFlow pays at least 5% with a 7-day cookie (its own FAQ); its Awin profile lists 5–8% and AOV of $1,000+. Jackery pays at least 5% (its own page), with a 30-day cookie on Awin US.
- Commission per sale is the highest of the group, but page one is held by Pop Sci, Outdoor Life, TechRadar, CNET, OutdoorGearLab, Bob Vila and BestReviews, all claiming hands-on testing.

**Pet supplies (rank 6)**
- Chewy pays 4% with a 15-day cookie on Partnerize and commissions existing-customer orders. Low $ per sale, saturated with competitors.

**Outdoor (rank 7)**
- REI pays 5% with a 15-day cookie on AvantLink. Backcountry and Osprey pay about 8% with 30-day cookies. Strong programs, but heavily seasonal and crowded.

## Top three

1. **Home water filtration (86.0).** Best combination of buyer intent, direct-program commissions (8–10%+), built-in repeat purchases, Facebook audience fit, and a clear trust gap to exploit (see competitor analysis).
2. **Home espresso & coffee gear (77.6).** Excellent video content and solid AOV, but the hardest to win against established reviewers without hands-on testing.
3. **3D printing (76.8).** Great long-tail SEO and strong repeat purchases (filament), but a weaker Facebook fit, and Facebook is our mandated primary channel.

## Final recommendation

**Launch in home water filtration**, positioned as a *certification-first* buying guide: every product claim is checked against the NSF, WQA and IAPMO certification listings. Rationale and risk controls are in BUSINESS_PLAN.md.

**Main risk:** health-adjacent claims. Mitigation: we never claim health outcomes. We report what a product is *certified* to reduce, link to the certifier's listing, and point readers to their utility's water quality report or a certified lab test.
