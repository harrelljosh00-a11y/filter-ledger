# Financial Model

_All figures are planning assumptions, not forecasts or guarantees._

## Formula
Monthly revenue = Visitors × Outbound click-through rate (CTR) × Merchant conversion rate × Average order value (AOV) × Commission rate

## Scenario assumptions
| Input | Conservative | Moderate | Optimistic | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outbound CTR (visitor → merchant) | 8% | 12% | 18% | Assumption for buying-intent pages; to be measured in GA4 |
| Merchant conversion | 2% | 3% | 4% | Waterdrop states a 3.5% average (merchant claim) |
| AOV | $150 | $180 | $220 | Aquasana case study $129; Waterdrop states >$235 |
| Commission | 6% | 8% | 9% | Blend of direct programs (7–10%) and Amazon (~3–4%) |
| **Revenue per 1,000 visitors** | **$14.40** | **$51.84** | **$142.56** | Computed |

Not modeled (upside): replacement-filter repeat purchases inside cookie windows, water-test-kit referrals, Facebook in-app affiliate earnings.
Not modeled (downside): returns and cancellations, Facebook traffic converting worse than search traffic, seasonality.

## Reading this
- At the moderate scenario, **$1,000/month needs ~19,000 visitors/month** and **$10,000/month needs ~193,000**. New sites typically take 6–12 months to reach the first of those numbers, if at all. Treat $25k/month as a long-horizon stretch.
- The biggest lever is **CTR × conversion**: pages that match the reader's specific water problem should beat generic "best of" lists. Measure this first.
