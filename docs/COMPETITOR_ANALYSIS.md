## Patterns among the winners

1. **Credibility signals carry the category.** Every strong player leans on lab data, certifications or credentials. Content without them reads as spam.
2. **Fear converts on social, and it backfires on trust.** Viral videos are microscope shots, dye tests and "your Brita is useless" claims. Tap Score explicitly debunks dye tests. A calm, evidence-first voice is underserved.
3. **Content is organized by filter type** (pitcher, faucet, under-sink, reverse osmosis, whole-house, shower) **and by contaminant** (lead, PFAS, chlorine/chloramine, fluoride, well water).
4. **Calls to action** fall into two groups: "test your water first" (Tap Score kits, often with affiliate discounts [V]) and "see our top pick".
5. **Big publishers update slowly.** TechGearLab's review was last updated in 2024. A site that updates monthly and logs its changes can compete on freshness. Water Filter Guru already publishes a change log [V], so that format works.

## Where Filter Ledger wins

| Gap | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Shoppers can't tell "certified" from "tested to" from marketing claims | **Claim Check** score: each claim is labeled *Certified* (with a link to the certifier listing), *Lab-tested, not certified*, or *Marketing claim only* |
| Fear-based social content | Calm 30–45 s explainers by Wren (AI host): one claim, one verdict, one link |
| Stale pages | Every page shows a "last verified" date and a change log; an automated weekly check finds stale certifications and broken links |
| Generic picks | Picks matched to the reader's own water report: "Your report shows chloramine → here's what's certified for it" |
| Ownership cost hidden | Cost-per-gallon and annual filter cost on every product, from the manufacturer's rated capacity |

## Honest limitation

Water Filter Guru runs real lab tests and we don't. We won't claim testing, and we won't out-test them. We compete on clarity, certification auditing, freshness, cost math, and social distribution. If revenue justifies it, a paid lab-testing program can be proposed later (PENDING_APPROVALS.md).
