## What we collect
- **Analytics:** if enabled, Google Analytics 4 collects usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type, clicks on outbound links) using cookies. We use it to see which pages are useful.
- **Affiliate links:** when you click an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate network may set cookies to credit the referral. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.
- **Email (future):** if you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address with our email provider so we can send it. You can unsubscribe from any email.
- **Contact:** if you email us, we keep your message to reply.

## What we don't do
We don't sell personal information. We don't run third-party ad networks on this site.

## Your choices
You can block or delete cookies in your browser. To ask about or delete data you've sent us, [contact us](/contact/).

## Changes
We'll update this page and its date when practices change.
