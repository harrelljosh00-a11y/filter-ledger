Filter Ledger is an independent buying guide for home water filters. Our job is simple: check what a filter is **officially certified** to do, show what it really **costs to own**, and help you match it to **your water**.

## What we do
- Check manufacturer claims against public certification listings from NSF, WQA and IAPMO.
- Label every claim **Certified**, **Lab-tested (not certified)** or **Claim only**.
- Calculate yearly filter costs from rated capacity and replacement prices.
- Date-stamp every page and keep a change log.

## What we don't do
- **We don't run lab tests**, and we never say we did. When we cite test data, we name who ran the test.
- We don't invent customer stories or personal experiences.
- We don't give medical advice. For health questions about your water, talk to your utility, your local health department or a doctor.
