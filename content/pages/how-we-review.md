## Claim Check
For every product we:
1. Record the exact model and replacement-cartridge numbers.
2. List every contaminant-reduction claim the manufacturer makes.
3. Look each claim up in the NSF, WQA and IAPMO public databases.
4. Label it:
   - {{verdict:certified}} on an accredited certifier's listing for that model and contaminant (linked).
   - {{verdict:lab-tested}} supported by a named third-party lab report, but not certified.
   - {{verdict:claim-only}} no certification or test data found.
5. Calculate cost of ownership: price ÷ rated gallons, plus yearly cartridge cost, using the manufacturer's numbers and dated prices.
6. Record the date. Pages are re-checked on a schedule, and products whose evidence is more than 90 days old are flagged for re-verification.

## What we don't claim
We don't do hands-on or lab testing. Rankings come from verified certifications, published specifications and cost math, never from invented experiences.
